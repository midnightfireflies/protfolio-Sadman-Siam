## Automated Lead Qualification & Follow-Up

An n8n workflow for a digital-marketing lead funnel. It receives form submissions, updates Google Sheets, sends a personalized thank-you email, and notifies the owner with a **Hot / Warm / Cold** lead classification.

**Stack:** n8n, Google Forms, Google Sheets, Gmail, automation logic.

## Subsequence Finder — LCS & LIS

A C-based algorithm laboratory project using dynamic programming to compute Longest Common Subsequence and Longest Increasing Subsequence results, including DP-table output and comparison metrics.

**Stack:** C, Dynamic Programming, Algorithms, Problem Solving.
